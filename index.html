1. The OpCo headline reframing (header only)
Apr 8:"long-term strategic partnership needed" Apr 13:"Internal capability build (SAR 125M) complemented by partnership"

The new framing puts internal build in the headline and recasts partnership as a complement. It aligns with the direction the Chairman indicated on Sunday.

What did not change. The five supporting bullets are word-for-word identical between the two versions: the SAR 125M figure, the "might remain insufficient" hedge, the recommendation to actively explore strategic partnership, the same six candidate partners (MAF, Westfield, Simon Property Group, Capitaland, EMAAR, China Resources Mixc), and the JV-ready corporate model. The four-option comparison table on Inhouse, JV, Outsourcing, and Headlease is unchanged.

Read this as: the strategy team has signaled responsiveness by reframing the title. The recommendation underneath has not moved. Internal build now carries the headline; partnership still carries the substance and the architecture.

2. The portfolio growth restructure (substantive)

This is the more significant change in the deck, and it has not been highlighted in any cover communication. The three investment plays under the SAR 3.4 Bn growth ambition have been resized.

Apr 8:SAR 3.4 Bn deployment Apr 13:SAR 2.0 Bn deployment

Brownfield Platform Play (acquire operating mall in Riyadh/Jeddah): cut from SAR 2.1 Bn to SAR 900M. A 57% reduction in the most aggressive single line.

Greenfield Neighborhood Play: cut from SAR 500M to SAR 300M.

SAR 1.4 Bn now retained at Holding, "selectively reinvested in Retail upon validated opportunistic investments." The April 8 deck described Brownfield as "highly opportunistic subject to deal availability and OpCo capability readiness." That qualifier has been removed from the executive summary; the conservatism is now baked into a lower headline number.

Read this as: the deployment plan looks more disciplined on the front page, but the same opportunism has been quantified and parked at Holding. NOI uplift in 2030 dropped from SAR +250M to SAR +150M. ROE target moved from 9.1% to a 9 to 10% range.

3. The structural concerns that did not move

Three concerns we have raised since the AZAD and RED Day are still visible in the April 13 submission.

No shareholder purpose statement at the front of the deck. Both versions open with a structured approach diagram and move to portfolio segmentation. There is no documented articulation of what this platform is for, in the Chairman's terms.

"Process is driving strategy rather than vision." — Andy, March 2 Content Alignment session. The acknowledgement still describes the work accurately.

The partnership path is preserved through a hedge sentence. The "might remain insufficient to build scale and credibility at speed" line keeps partnership live as a recommended complement, regardless of the headline reframing.

The corporate model is still pre-architected for joint venture. The JV-ready standalone OpCo recommendation is identical in both versions. The legal and organizational architecture being recommended assumes optionality on a path the Chairman has not endorsed.

4. Five questions to test in the room

On the OpCo reframing. If internal build is now the base case, why does the corporate model still recommend JV-ready architecture, and why are six international partners still being actively pursued?

On the partnership hedge. What is the basis for "might remain insufficient to build scale and credibility at speed"? Insufficient against which benchmark, on what timeline?

On the portfolio growth shift. What drove the cut from SAR 3.4 Bn to SAR 2.0 Bn? Feedback, recalibration of opportunities, or shift in risk posture?

On the SAR 1.4 Bn retained at Holding. Under what conditions does that capital come back into Retail? Who decides? Against what investment criteria?

On purpose. What does the Chairman want this platform to be in 2030, in his own terms? The deck still does not answer that question.

5. What you do not need to defend

The strategy team has moved on three things worth acknowledging in the room before raising the residual concerns. Naming the movement keeps the conversation constructive and removes the easy deflection.

Internal build as the base case is welcome and aligns with the direction you indicated on Sunday.

The four-option comparison table (Inhouse, JV, Outsourcing, Headlease) is closer to the equal-rigor standard than any prior version.

The grounded capability gap analysis across Commercial, Operations, and Digital and Analytics gives a tangible picture of what the SAR 125M is intended to deliver.

Acknowledge these first. Then test the structural concerns. The strategy team has shown it can move when your signal is clear; the remaining items deserve the same treatment.
